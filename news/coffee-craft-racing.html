Coffee Craft Racing: From Country Roads to City Streets

Race through town and the countryside, stop for coffee, and unlock a new set of wheels.

November 17, 2026

Coffee Craft Racing is a casual 3D browser racing game created by Daniel McKeown. It takes the map of Coffee Craft and the surrounding countryside in a new direction: get behind the wheel and race a driverless robo-taxi through country roads and city streets.

From the forest to the pier

The first course starts on a quiet forest highway, heads through a pink haze into town, and finishes out at the pier. Other playable routes include a mountain descent leading onto an Interstate and a highway bridge over town, and an island course through a Norwegian village, pine islands, and neighborhood streets.

Coffee, boosts, and new cars

Slow down near a roadside coffee stand to pull in, grab a drink, and chat with the barista. A carried coffee can also give you a five-second boost on the road. Race results earn points toward unlocking the Skyview CUV, City Bus, and Redwood RS sports car, with Normal and Challenging difficulty settings to choose from.

Play in your browser

Coffee Craft Racing supports desktop and mobile browsers, with keyboard controls and an on-screen pad. Use the arrow keys or WASD to steer, accelerate, and brake, and press the apostrophe key for a coffee boost or to pull into a stand. The game is intended for players 18 and over.

The source code is available on GitHub under the ISC License. The game runs from a static web server with no build step; PHP is optional for accounts, server saves, and AI features.
