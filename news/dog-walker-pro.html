Dog Walker Pro: A Small City Full of Animals

Take a walk, meet the neighbors, and explore a pixel-art world with dogs and birds by your side.

November 7, 2026

Dog Walker Pro is a top-down, open-world game created by Daniel McKeown. Set in a small pixel-art city, it invites players to walk dogs and birds, chat with characters around town, and make time for a coffee or a new hat.

A walk around town

Players can explore the city and interact with its residents while caring for animals. During a walk, they can pet or encourage their animal companions. Away from the walk, there are hats to change and coffee to buy.

Play your way

The game runs in a browser and supports keyboard and touch controls. Move with the arrow keys or WASD, use E to interact, X to change hats or encourage an animal, and T or / to talk to a nearby character. An on-screen controller is available for touch play.

Built in the open

Dog Walker Pro is available to play online, and its source code is published on GitHub under the ISC License. The game uses JavaScript modules and a canvas for its front end, with a PHP backend for accounts and saves.
