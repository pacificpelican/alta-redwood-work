Texas Data: Three Ways to Work with Local AI

Organize documents by region, ask questions, and experiment with story continuations—all from one local-first app.

November 13, 2026

Texas Data is a lightweight PHP app for keeping project files organized and trying out AI workflows. Its Texas map groups uploads into regions, which you can rename to fit your work. The app uses local file storage by default, with MySQL available as an option.

Three AI tools, three different uses

The AI Assistant works with documents you upload: choose a region, then ask for help analyzing or summarizing its files. The LLM Chat is for open-ended prompts, with a personal history so you can revisit earlier answers. And the Shake-speare Prediction Machine takes a passage and asks the model to continue it, with runs saved to your history.

Local by design

All three features connect to an Ollama server on your own machine, rather than sending prompts to a cloud AI service. You can choose the model in the app’s configuration. An account keeps your profile, settings, uploads, and AI history together; email signup is available, with Google sign-in as an optional configuration.

Keep a copy of a conversation

LLM chats and Shakespeare predictions have printable permalink pages. Open a saved conversation to print it or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Built in the open

Texas Data is published on GitHub under the ISC License. The project README has the setup details, including how to run the app locally and connect it to Ollama.
