Coffee Craft: Coffee and Community in a Rainy City

Run coffee shops, explore the neighborhood, and get to know the people and places around you.

November 9, 2026

Coffee Craft is a casual, third-person open-world browser game created by Daniel McKeown. Set in a damp Pacific Northwest International District, it combines running coffee shops with exploring a lively city neighborhood.

Explore the neighborhood

Beyond the coffee shops, players can visit restaurants, talk with neighbors, and wander among a streetcar, an old train station, and a park with a pickleball court. The city has people and animals to meet, as well as activities such as coffee roasting and espresso-making.

Play your way

Coffee Craft works in desktop and mobile browsers. Move with the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen pad; use / to talk, . to interact, and ' for friendly greetings and entering doors. The game is intended for players 18 and over.

Built in the open

The game's source code is available on GitHub. Its front end uses JavaScript modules, and a PHP backend supports accounts and saves. The game can be played without AI, which is an optional enhancement.
