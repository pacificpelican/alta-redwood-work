Coffee Craft: Coffee and Community in a Rainy City

Run coffee shops, explore the neighborhood, and get to know the people and places around you.

November 9, 2026

Coffee Craft is a casual, third-person open-world browser game created by Daniel McKeown. Set in a damp Pacific Northwest International District, it combines running coffee shops with exploring a lively city neighborhood.

Explore the neighborhood

Beyond the coffee shops, players can visit restaurants, talk with neighbors, ride a streetcar, and explore an old train station and a park with a pickleball court. The city has people and animals to meet, as well as activities such as coffee roasting and espresso-making.

Food, coffee, and business

Players can sample coffee and food from around the neighborhood, from coffee shops to restaurants serving ramen and Korean donuts. They can also operate their own businesses, buy properties, and renovate businesses that have been liquidated. Whether exploring the local food scene or building a business, there is plenty to do between cups of coffee.

Built in the open

Play Coffee Craft on the game website in a desktop or mobile browser. The game is intended for players 18 and over.

For a geek option, the game's source code is available on GitHub, with instructions for running it on your own machine. The game can run from a static web server; PHP supports accounts and server saves. AI is an optional enhancement, not a requirement for play.
