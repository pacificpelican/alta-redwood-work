Dinosaurs and Friends Zoo: A Cretaceous Visit with New Discoveries

Meet Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brontosaurus, and feathered friends—then fund research to discover more.

November 25, 2026

Dinosaurs and Friends Zoo is a browser game by Daniel McKeown that invites you to visit a zoo of dinosaurs—and the very small dinosaurs known as parakeets. Tour the habitats, get a closer look at the residents, and settle in for a visit.

Meet the residents

Stop by exhibits for Triceratops, Stegosaurus, and Brontosaurus, then visit the Parakeet House. You can peer through viewing windows, feed the small dinosaurs in the petting pen, and ride the cable car around the zoo. Fund research at the Genome Lab to rediscover or synthesize additional species as you play.

Talk and make friends

Visitors, fans, and zoo staff are ready to chat. Strike up a conversation and join a group to keep talking together. The zoo also has plenty to discover between conversations, from a lagoon-side soda stand to the parked safari truck.

Play online or locally

Play in a browser with keyboard or touch controls. The game can run from a static file server for local play, saving to your browser. An online PHP setup can add accounts and server saves, with optional AI-enhanced dialogue through a configured Ollama or cloud service; rule-based dialogue works without those services.

The source code and README are available on GitHub, including setup details and the full controls list.
